## Presentation recommendation

Keep the business's confirmed list prices and show “USD / tonne” beside each amount. Label packing separately: “Big bag”, “18 kg bags”, “Cage”. Show ex-factory, VAT/freight exclusions and international MOQ together near prices. Quote requests should include product, stone size, packing, tonnes and destination. Public evidence cannot substantiate “lowest price” or “best price guaranteed”; the buyer's relevant comparison is a written quote for the same grade and delivered cost. Avoid currency conversions without a dated exchange-rate source.
