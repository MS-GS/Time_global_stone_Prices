A LITTLE MATERIAL KNOW-HOW

The right stone, in the right place.

Think about the feeling you want to create, then the practical details. Here’s a good place to start.

01 /

Set the mood

White stones brighten borders. Dark tones add definition. Mixed river pebbles bring a softer, more organic rhythm to dry stream beds and planted spaces.

02 /

Match the application

Use decorative pebbles for surface cover and accents. Pumice can improve air space in growing mixes; lava brings a porous texture to landscape beds. Grade and installation matter.

03 /

See it in person

Natural colour, size and texture vary, especially when wet. Ask about a representative sample. Confirm suitability before use in water features, aquariums or specialist construction.
