HELP WITH YOUR CHOICE

A few tips before you choose.

Start with the look you like, then think about where the stone will go. These tips can help you narrow it down.

01 /

Start with the colour

White stones brighten garden borders. Dark stones stand out against light paving. Mixed river pebbles give planted areas and dry stream beds a softer, natural look.

02 /

Think about the use

Decorative pebbles work well as surface cover and garden details. Pumice can add air space to growing mixes, while lava gives garden beds a rough, porous texture. Choose the right size and installation for the job.

03 /

Ask about a sample

Natural stones vary in colour, size and texture, and can look different when wet. Ask for a sample that represents your order. Check suitability before using them in water features, aquariums or specialist building work.

Where this advice comes from+

The sources below helped us put together these general tips. Our colour names describe the look of the stones; they do not confirm the mineral type. The right choice depends on the stone supplied, its size and how it is installed.
